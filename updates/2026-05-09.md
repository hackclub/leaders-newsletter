In the before times, leaders received 🍕 food grants 🍔 when completing a workshop. These grants can now be repurposed for other club uses as Club Grants. Use them in a variety of ways, as long as they are for your participants! Here are some ways you can use them ↴

### Spring Bling Event! 🌿 🌸
 
From now through June, help everyone prepare for spring by making custom keychains, medallions, or other unique 'danglies' *(not sure if I made up a word there...)*! 
 
Earn 💸 Club Grants 💸 during this event to finish the school year strong with your club! 
 
Earn more by building more:
- 💵 1 project = $4
- 💵 2 projects = $6
- 💵 3 projects = $8 
<i>*per person!</i>
