### It's Campfire Time 🏕️!

Campfire is a game jam happening this February in 200 cities all over the world! Game jams are just like hackathons, where you build games! If you remember me from the last newsletter, I was at Daydream, which was another game jam by Hack Club! 
 
It was really awesome, and as someone who has organized multiple hackathons before, I recommend you organize one too! You will learn a lot; from how to plan an event, reach out to sponsors, budget, and SO MUCH MORE. It’s really fun, especially if you organize one with your club. 
 
Of course, you don’t have to organize one - you can RSVP to attend the closest one to you! Game jams are magical experiences.
 
-Lynn

## General Clubs Updates 

Since it has been a while, here are a few important things to keep you updated!

### Club Levels 🎚️
*We want clubs who are building awesome projects to get more.* 💪 If your members ship projects to Hack Club programs, more perks unlock to make meetings better! Here's a preview of perks at the first two levels:

<u>**Level One**</u>
- Every active club starts here
- Added to the Hack Club map
- Bi-weekly newsletters
- Support from the Clubs Team
- CodeCrafter's membership

<u>**Level Two**</u>
- Perks from Level One
- Monthly Club Mail - Stickers, postcards, or other items to pass out in meetings
- Sticker Orders

**How do I level up my club up?** Get people building! Shipping to any official Hack Club YSWS program counts.

<hr>

### Leader's Portal 🛜

This is becoming a one stop shop for your club. Use it for:
- Contacting your Hack Club ambassador
- Updating club & leader information
- Finding YSWS programs for meetings
- Tracking shipped projects from your club
 
Visit the Leader's Portal [here](https://leaders.hackclub.com/)!

### Hack Club Spaces (v2) 💻
*GitHub or some other platform blocked 🚫 🤬 in your school?* Use Hack Club Spaces to build within your browser!

**Features**
- Create cloud development environments
- Use VSCode, Blender, Kicad, or Godot in a web browser
- Full terminal access to develop any type of program you want

**Clubs**
- Full club integration
- Automatic member linking (invite members using the Leader's Portal)
- Easily share spaces so leaders can provide support

Check out Hack Club Spaces [here](https://spaces.hackclub.com/)!
